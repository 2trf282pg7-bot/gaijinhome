Frequently Asked Questions

Can foreigners rent apartments in Japan? ▾
Yes, but it requires more documentation and often a guarantor or guarantor company.
What documents do I need? ▾
Residence card, passport, employment contract or enrollment certificate, income proof, and emergency contact details.
How much does it really cost to move in? ▾
Typically 3–6 months of rent upfront including key money, deposit, agency fee, and insurance.
What is key money (礼金)? ▾
A non-refundable gift to the landlord, usually 1–2 months' rent. Many zero-key-money apartments exist.
Can I rent without a guarantor? ▾
Yes. Guarantor companies, share houses, UR housing, and foreigner-specialist agencies all offer paths without a personal guarantor.
