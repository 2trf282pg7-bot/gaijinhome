Finding an apartment in Osaka as a foreigner doesn't have to be the nightmare you've heard about. While Tokyo often dominates the conversation about expat life in Japan, Osaka has quietly become one of the most foreigner-friendly cities for renting, with lower costs, friendlier landlords, and a growing number of agencies that specialize in helping international residents find homes.

Having helped thousands of foreigners navigate the Japanese rental market, we've put together this comprehensive guide specifically for Osaka — covering everything from the best neighborhoods to the exact documents you'll need, and how to secure an apartment even without a Japanese guarantor.

Why Osaka is Great for Foreign Renters

Osaka offers several distinct advantages over Tokyo when it comes to apartment hunting as a foreigner:

  • 30-40% lower rent compared to equivalent apartments in Tokyo's 23 wards
  • More relaxed landlords — Osaka's famous friendly culture extends to property owners
  • Growing international community — over 250,000 registered foreign residents as of 2025
  • Excellent public transport — anywhere is accessible within 30-40 minutes
  • Lower key money requirements — many Osaka apartments charge 0-1 month instead of Tokyo's typical 1-2 months
💡 Good to Know: Osaka landlords have a reputation for being more negotiable on initial costs compared to their Tokyo counterparts. Don't be afraid to ask about reducing key money or deposit, especially if you can show stable income.

Best Foreigner-Friendly Neighborhoods in Osaka

Not all Osaka neighborhoods are equally welcoming to foreign tenants. Here are the areas where you'll find the most foreigner-friendly options:

🏙️ Namba / Shinsaibashi

The heart of Osaka's entertainment and shopping district. Extremely convenient with multiple train lines, abundant restaurants, and a vibrant nightlife. Most landlords here are accustomed to foreign tenants due to the tourist-heavy nature of the area.

Best for: Those who want to be in the center of everything and don't mind the bustle

1K: ¥65,000-95,000/month

🚄 Shin-Osaka

The Shinkansen hub makes this perfect for business travelers or anyone who frequently travels. Modern apartment buildings, good supermarkets, and quick access to both central Osaka and Kyoto. Very foreigner-friendly due to the international business crowd.

Best for: Business professionals, frequent travelers, families

1K: ¥55,000-75,000/month

🌳 Tennoji / Abeno

A great balance of urban convenience and residential calm. Home to Tennoji Park and Abeno Harukas (Japan's tallest building). Increasingly popular with foreigners due to reasonable rents and excellent access to Namba and the airport.

Best for: Budget-conscious renters, those seeking work-life balance

1K: ¥45,000-65,000/month

🎓 Umeda / Kita

Osaka's business district with sleek office towers and upscale shopping. More expensive but offers premium apartments and is where many international companies have offices. Great for professionals who want a short commute.

Best for: Corporate professionals, those working in Kita area

1K: ¥70,000-100,000/month

🏠 Higashi-Osaka / Fuse

More residential and local-feeling than central areas. Significantly cheaper rents and still decent train access. Some landlords may be less experienced with foreigners, but the savings can be substantial.

Best for: Budget hunters, those seeking authentic local life

1K: ¥35,000-50,000/month

Initial Move-in Costs

The upfront costs are where many foreigners get surprised. For a typical ¥60,000/month apartment in Osaka:

  • First month's rent: ¥60,000
  • Deposit (敷金/shikikin): ¥60,000-120,000 (1-2 months)
  • Key money (礼金/reikin): ¥0-60,000 (0-1 month — often negotiable in Osaka)
  • Agency fee: ¥60,000 (typically 1 month + tax)
  • Guarantor company fee: ¥30,000-60,000 (50-100% of 1 month)
  • Fire insurance: ¥15,000-20,000
  • Lock change fee: ¥15,000-25,000
  • Cleaning fee: ¥25,000-40,000 (sometimes paid at move-out)

💰 Total Initial Cost Estimate

For a ¥60,000/month apartment: ¥265,000 - ¥385,000
That's roughly 4-6 months' rent upfront. Budget accordingly!
